A garage door panel can be damaged in a moment: a car bumper, a stray ball or a storm. Whether you should repair the panel, replace one section or replace the whole door depends on the type of damage, the door's age and whether matching parts are still available.

## Types of panel damage

- **Small dents** in steel panels, with no cracks or bends at the edges
- **Large dents or creases**, especially across a panel's ribs
- **Cracks or splits** in steel, wood, fiberglass or composite panels
- **Bent sections** after a vehicle impact, often affecting the hinges, rollers and tracks too
- **Rot, warping or rust** that weakens the panel

## Why damaged panels matter

A bent panel is not only a cosmetic problem. It can:

- Stop the door from running smoothly in its tracks
- Put extra strain on the springs, cables and opener
- Let in water, cold air and pests
- Make the door less secure

If the door is crooked or off track after an impact, stop using it. See [garage door off track](/blog/garage-door-off-track/).

## When repair may be enough

- The damage is limited to one panel
- The panel is not cracked or bent at the joints
- The door still runs smoothly and is balanced
- Hinges, rollers and tracks are undamaged, or can be replaced on their own

Small dents in steel doors can sometimes be reduced, though rarely made invisible.

## When to replace the section

Replacing a single section is often the best choice when one panel is badly dented, cracked or bent and the rest of the door is in good condition. The key question is whether a **matching replacement section** is available for your door's brand, model, size, style and colour. A technician can usually find out from the door's labels and measurements.

## When a new door makes more sense

- Several panels are damaged
- Matching sections are no longer made
- The door has rust, rot or warping beyond one panel
- The door is old, heavy or poorly insulated, and you were considering an upgrade anyway

Our guide to [repairing or replacing your garage door](/blog/repair-or-replace-garage-door/) covers this decision in more detail.

## Do not try to straighten panels yourself

Hammering or forcing a bent section can crack it or damage the hinges and rollers. A badly bent section can also put the door out of balance. Removing a section means working near springs and cables under high tension, which is a job for a trained technician.
