Rollers are the small wheels that carry your garage door along its tracks. They are easy to overlook, but worn rollers are one of the most common causes of a noisy, shaky door. When it is time to replace them, the main choice is nylon or steel.

## Nylon rollers

Nylon rollers have a nylon wheel, and better-quality ones have sealed ball bearings inside.

- **Quieter**, which is the biggest reason homeowners choose them
- **Smooth running**, especially with sealed bearings
- **Need less lubrication** than steel rollers
- Cheap nylon or plastic rollers without bearings wear out faster

## Steel rollers

Steel rollers have a metal wheel, usually with exposed ball bearings.

- **Strong and durable**, which suits heavy doors
- **Louder**, because metal runs on a metal track
- **Need regular lubrication**, and can rust in damp garages

## Which should you choose?

For most homes, **nylon rollers with sealed ball bearings** give the best balance of quiet running and long life. Steel rollers can make sense on very heavy doors or where noise does not matter. A technician can recommend rollers that suit your door's weight and how often you use it.

## Signs your rollers need replacing

- Grinding, squeaking or rattling as the door moves
- Shaky or jerky movement
- Cracked, chipped or flat-spotted wheels
- Rollers that wobble on their stems
- The opener straining to move the door

Left alone, badly worn rollers can cause the door to jump its track. See [garage door off track](/blog/garage-door-off-track/) and [how to quiet a noisy garage door](/blog/noisy-garage-door/).

## Leave the bottom rollers to a professional

The bottom roller brackets are connected to the lift cables, which are under high tension from the springs. Removing them without the right training can cause serious injury. Leave roller replacement, especially on the bottom section, to a trained technician.
