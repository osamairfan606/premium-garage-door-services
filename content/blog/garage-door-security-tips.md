For many homes, the garage door is the largest entrance to the house, and the door from the garage often leads straight inside. A few simple habits and upgrades make it much harder for anyone to get in.

## 10 garage door security tips

### 1. Keep the door closed

It sounds obvious, but an open garage door shows what is inside and offers an easy way in. If you often forget to close it, consider an opener with open-door alerts or a timer that closes the door automatically.

### 2. Protect the emergency release

The emergency release lets you open the door by hand during a power cut. On some doors it can be reached from outside through the top of the door. An emergency release shield, fitted by a technician, makes this much harder without stopping you from using the release inside.

### 3. Do not leave the remote in your car

A remote left in a car parked outside gives anyone who breaks into the car a way into the garage. Use a keychain remote, a keypad or a smartphone app instead.

### 4. Lock the door between the garage and the house

Treat it like a front door, with a solid door and a deadbolt.

### 5. Use rolling-code remotes

Very old openers use fixed codes that are easier to copy. Modern openers change the code every time you press the remote.

### 6. Change the keypad code

Change the code if you have shared it with contractors, guests or previous owners, and avoid easy codes such as 1234 or your house number.

### 7. Use lock or vacation mode when you are away

Many wall controls can block remotes and keypads while you are away.

### 8. Cover or frost garage windows

Frosted film or blinds stop people seeing whether your car is home and what is stored inside.

### 9. Add lighting

Motion-sensor lights over the garage door, and an opener with a built-in light, make the area less inviting at night.

### 10. Keep the door in good repair

Worn rollers, damaged panels or a door that does not close fully can leave gaps. Regular maintenance keeps the door closing properly. See [what is included in a garage door tune-up](/blog/garage-door-tune-up-what-is-included/).
