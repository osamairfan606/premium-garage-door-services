Most garage door problems can be repaired: springs, cables, rollers, openers and single panels are all replaceable parts. Sometimes, though, repeated repairs cost more than they are worth, and a new door is the better investment. Here is how to decide.

## When repair is usually the right choice

- **A single part has failed**, such as a spring, cable, roller, hinge or the opener
- **One panel is damaged** and a matching replacement section is available. See [panel repair](/services/panel-repair/).
- The door is **structurally sound**, with no widespread rust, rot or warping
- You are **happy with how it looks** and how well it insulates
- The door has had **few problems** in the past

## When replacement is worth considering

- **Several panels are damaged**, or a matching replacement panel is no longer made
- **Rust, rot or warping** affects the door's strength
- You are paying for **repairs again and again**
- The door is **very heavy or poorly insulated**, and the garage is hard to heat or cool
- You want **better curb appeal** before selling, or a style that suits a renovated home
- The door or opener lacks modern **safety features**

## Questions to ask before deciding

1. **What exactly is wrong, and what would the repair fix?** A good technician explains the cause, not just the symptom.
2. **Are other parts close to failing?** Springs, cables and rollers wear together.
3. **Is a matching panel still available?**
4. **What would a new door cost, compared with the repairs likely in the next few years?**
5. **What insulation and style options would a new door give me?**

Ask for both options in writing so you can compare them side by side.

## Choosing a new door

If replacement makes sense, think about:

- **Material:** steel, wood or composite, depending on looks, maintenance and climate
- **Insulation:** worth it for attached or heated garages in cold Midwest winters
- **Style:** traditional raised panel, carriage house or modern flush and glass designs
- **Windows and colour:** to match the rest of the home
- **The opener:** a new door is a good time to upgrade to a quieter, safer opener
