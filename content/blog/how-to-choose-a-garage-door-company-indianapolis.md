A broken garage door can trap your car inside or leave your home open. In Indianapolis there are many companies to choose from, and not all of them give fair quotes. Use this checklist to choose a garage door company you can trust.

## Check that the company is genuinely local

A local company should have an Indianapolis phone number, a clear business name and a Google Business Profile with real reviews. Be wary of ads that lead to a national call centre that passes your job to whoever is available.

## Read reviews properly

Look past the star rating. Useful reviews describe the problem, how quickly the technician arrived and whether the price matched the estimate. Check how the company responds to negative reviews too.

## Insist on a written estimate

Before any work begins, the technician should explain the problem, show you the worn or broken part and give you a written estimate. A trustworthy company is happy to explain the options, including when a simple repair is enough.

## Ask about warranties

Ask what warranty covers the parts and the labour, and keep it in writing with your invoice.

## Ask about insurance

Springs, cables and heavy door sections can cause damage or injury if handled badly. Ask whether the company carries insurance.

## Make sure they handle emergencies

Freeze-thaw cycles and cold snaps in central Indiana are hard on springs and openers. Check whether the company offers 24/7 emergency service and same-day appointments. See [why garage door springs break in cold weather](/blog/why-garage-door-springs-break-in-cold-weather/).

## Red flags

- A low phone price that grows once the technician is on site
- Unmarked vehicles and no business name on the paperwork
- Pressure to replace the whole door or opener without a clear reason
- Cash-only payment
- No written estimate or warranty

## Questions to ask before you book

- Do you charge for the estimate?
- Will I get the price in writing before you start?
- What warranty comes with the parts and labour?
- How soon can someone come out?
