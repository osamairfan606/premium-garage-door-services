A smart garage door opener lets you check and control your garage door from your phone. It is useful if you have ever driven away wondering whether you closed the door, or need to let in a family member or a delivery while you are out. Here is what to look for, and whether to upgrade the opener or add a smart kit to the one you have.

## What "smart" usually means

Most smart openers and add-on kits offer:

- **App control** to open and close the door from anywhere with an internet connection
- **Open/closed status**, so you always know where the door is
- **Alerts** when the door opens, closes or is left open
- **Schedules** that close the door automatically at a set time
- **Activity history** showing who used the door and when
- **Shared access** for family members, with separate logins

## Features worth paying attention to

### Reliable Wi-Fi in the garage

Garages often have weak Wi-Fi. Check the signal where the opener is mounted before you buy, or plan for a range extender.

### Safety for remote closing

Remote closing works with the opener's safety sensors and usually gives a warning light and beep before the door moves. These features exist because nobody can see the doorway when the door is closed remotely. Never disable them. See [how safety sensors work](/blog/garage-door-safety-sensors/).

### Battery backup

A battery backup lets the door open during a power cut, which matters in winter storms.

### Security

Look for rolling-code remotes, two-factor sign-in for the app, and the ability to remove users or remotes quickly. If you have lost a remote, read [how to reprogram a garage door remote](/blog/reprogram-garage-door-remote/).

### Compatibility

Check whether the opener or kit works with the voice assistants, smart-home systems or car integration you already use. Some features need a subscription, so check before buying.

## Upgrade the opener or add a smart kit?

**An add-on smart kit** can be a good choice if your opener is fairly new, quiet and reliable, and has safety sensors.

**A new smart opener** makes more sense if your opener is old, noisy or unreliable, has no safety sensors, or you also want features such as a quieter belt drive, battery backup or a wall-mount design. Compare the types in [belt vs. chain vs. wall-mount openers](/blog/belt-vs-chain-garage-door-opener/).

## Check the door first

Smart features cannot fix a door that is out of balance. A door with worn springs or cables strains any opener, smart or not. A technician should check the door's balance, springs and rollers when installing a new opener.
