A garage door that has come off its track is one of the most dangerous garage door problems. The rollers that keep the door steady are no longer guided, so the door can twist, jam or fall. **If your door is off track, stop using it and keep everyone clear.**

## How to tell if your door is off track

- One or more rollers are **outside the track**
- The door is **crooked**, with one side higher than the other
- There is a gap between the door and the track
- The door made a loud scraping or popping noise, then stopped
- The door moves unevenly or sticks partway

## Common causes

### Something hit the door

A car bumper, bike or lawnmower can bend a track or knock rollers out of line.

### Worn or broken rollers

Old rollers with worn bearings can wobble and jump out of the track. See [roller repair](/services/roller-repair/).

### Loose or misaligned tracks

Vibration loosens track brackets over time. If the tracks separate or tilt, the rollers can slip out.

### A broken cable or spring

When a [cable snaps](/blog/garage-door-cable-snapped/) or a [spring breaks](/blog/broken-garage-door-spring-signs-and-what-to-do/), one side of the door loses support. The door tilts and pulls the rollers out of the track.

### Obstructions

Dirt, debris or ice in the track can stop a roller and push it out.

## Why you should not keep using it

- **The door can fall.** An off-track door is no longer properly supported.
- **More damage.** Running the opener can bend panels and tracks or burn out the motor.
- **Injury risk.** Trying to force rollers back in, or loosening track bolts, can release a heavy door suddenly. Springs and cables are under high tension.

## What to do right now

1. Unplug the opener so nobody can press the button.
2. Keep people, pets and cars away from the door.
3. Do not lift, push or pull the door, and do not loosen any brackets.
4. If the door is stuck open, secure the door between the garage and the house.
5. Call a technician. Our [emergency guide](/blog/emergency-garage-door-repair-guide/) explains what to tell them.

## What a professional off-track repair involves

During an [off-track repair](/services/off-track-repair/), a technician will usually:

1. Secure the door and release tension where needed
2. Straighten or replace bent tracks and tighten their brackets
3. Put the rollers back in the track, replacing worn ones
4. Check the cables, drums and springs, and repair them if they caused the problem
5. Balance the door and test the opener's safety reverse

## Preventing it from happening again

- Have worn rollers replaced before they fail
- Keep the tracks clear of debris and ice
- Fix a crooked or jerky door early
- Book a yearly [tune-up](/services/maintenance-tune-up/)
