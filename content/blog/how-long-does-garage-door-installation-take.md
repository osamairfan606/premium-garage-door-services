Many homeowners want to know how long they will be without a working garage door. Installing a standard residential door is often done in a single visit. The total timeline also includes the estimate and, for many doors, the time it takes for the door to arrive.

## The steps from estimate to install

### 1. Onsite estimate

A technician measures the opening, checks the headroom, side room and framing, and talks through styles, insulation and openers. See [what to expect from a free garage door estimate](/blog/free-garage-door-estimate-what-to-expect/).

### 2. Ordering the door

Some doors in common sizes and styles may be available quickly. Custom sizes, special colours, windows and wood doors usually take longer to arrive. Your installer will tell you the expected lead time for the door you choose.

### 3. Install day

Replacing a standard single or double door, including removing the old one, is often completed in a few hours to a day. Adding a new opener adds some time.

## What can make install day longer

- **Removing the old door and springs**, especially old extension springs or a heavy wood door
- **Repairs to the frame or opening**, such as rotted wood or damaged jambs
- **A new opener** or new wiring
- **Custom or heavy doors**, such as large wood or glass doors
- **More than one door**

## What happens on install day

1. The old door, springs and tracks are safely removed
2. The opening and frame are checked
3. The new sections, tracks, springs and cables are installed
4. The door is balanced, and the opener is installed or connected
5. Safety sensors and auto-reverse are tested
6. You are shown how to use the door, opener and remotes

## How to prepare

- Clear a space inside the garage around the door and along the ceiling
- Move cars out of the garage and driveway
- Make sure there is a working outlet near the opener
- Keep children and pets away from the work area

## Why installation needs a professional

New doors are heavy, and the springs that balance them hold enormous tension. Removing old springs is one of the most dangerous parts of the job. Professional installation also means the door is balanced correctly, which protects the opener and helps the door last.
